**Hints without shrinking the board.** Sudoku hints now open in a slide-over panel instead of pushing the board smaller, with a clear step-by-step reveal, a swipe-away dismissal, and a 10-second cooldown before another hint.

Leaderboard results now show how many hints were used, such as **No hints** or **2 hints**. The count is reported beside the result and never changes a player's rank.
